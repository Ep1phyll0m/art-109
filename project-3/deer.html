When?

I receive visits from you frequently;
Though you seldom remain present, I’ve seen.
With your peers, you seem to act differently,
With me, simple pleasantries are routine.

Nine months, then 18 years I’ve cared for you.
We’ve had our fair share of disagreements,
But still, it is you who I most value.
In life, you are my one true contentment

Yet you refuse to look me in the eye.
I only ask for reciprocation:
It wouldn’t hurt, making an effort to try.
Am I just not worth consideration?

Must it go on until the very end?
Once I’ve fade away, Will you love me then?


Go again?
